# Problem Statement

     - Telecom companies lose revenue when customers stop using their services (this is called churn).

     - The goal is to predict whether a customer will churn or not based on their service usage and personal details.

# Objective 

     1. Analyze customer data

     2. Identify factors influencing churn

     3. Build a machine learning model to predict churn

     4. Evaluate model performance

     5. Prepare the model for deployment

# Final ML Objective -

   To perform binary classification, where:

        1 → Customer will churn

        0 → Customer will not churn

# Why Binary Classification?

      * The target variable Churn has only two possible values: Yes or No

      * Historical labeled data is available

      * The goal is prediction, not grouping or trend analysis
